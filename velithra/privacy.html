Privacy Policy — Veli'Thra qo'choH

Last updated: November 17, 2026
Contact: crumbzdevops26@gmail.com

Veli'Thra qo'choH is a browser extension that lets you rearrange a website's interface in your own browser. This policy explains, in plain language, what the extension does with data. The short version: your layouts stay on your own computer, and the extension makes one network call, only when you ask it to.

What stays on your device

Everything you arrange — control positions, labels, colors, sizes, tray membership, and notes — is stored only in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your own computer. It is never uploaded, synced, shared, or sold. If you uninstall the extension, the browser removes that stored data with it.

The one network call: license validation

If you buy Pro, you receive a license key. Entering the key in the extension's options page and tapping "Validate" sends one request to the Lemon Squeezy license API (api.lemonsqueezy.com) to check the key. That request carries only the license key and a random per-browser instance name generated on your device. It happens only when you tap Validate — there are no background checks, no periodic re-checks, and no other network calls in the extension.

Lemon Squeezy handles Pro purchases and is the merchant of record. Payment details are handled by Lemon Squeezy and are never seen by this extension. See Lemon Squeezy's own privacy policy for how they handle purchase data.

What we do not collect

  • No personal data: no name, email, address, or account of any kind.
  • No browsing history or page content. The extension only reads the controls on a page it is arranging, and that information never leaves your browser.
  • No analytics, no trackers, no advertising identifiers.
  • No accounts. The extension works without signing in to anything.

What we never do with data

We do not sell, rent, share, or trade any data, because we do not collect any. There is nothing to sell.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes will also be noted in the extension's release notes.
