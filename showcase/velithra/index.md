## Try it — everything below is movable

Drag any card, button, link, picture, or field to move it. Tap one to open its editor and resize, recolor, or rename it. Your arrangement saves to your own browser — come back and it's still where you left it. Veli'Thra runs on this page only: nothing is installed anywhere.

## The hero card

This whole card is draggable — grab it anywhere and pull it somewhere new. The button inside it moves on its own, too.

### Drag

Pick a piece up and put it where you want it. It stays there — even after a reload.

### Resize

Tap a piece to open its editor, then make it bigger, smaller, wider — your call.

### Recolor

The editor's color tools restyle any piece. The page becomes yours, one piece at a time.
