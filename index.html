Veli’Thra qo’choH

Reality, rearranged. Veli’Thra is a browser extension that lets you move, resize, recolor, and rename the controls on any website — in your own browser, on your own machine. Your layouts save only to your own computer. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is shared, and the website itself is never changed.

Get it on the Chrome Web Store Free — 1 website, install it in one tap. Try the demo Drag, resize, recolor — live on the demo page. Privacy policy What Veli’Thra collects, in plain words.
