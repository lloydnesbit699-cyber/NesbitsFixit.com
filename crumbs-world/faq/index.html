Can't find something?

Help for Crumbs World players: getting in, playing, creating, and what to do when a program or feature you want is nowhere to be found.

Send a request — start here

Tell us what you were looking for

If you wanted a program, tool, button, place, or feature and could not find it, send a request. Requests are read and grouped, and they help decide what gets built next.

Can't Find It Request

The form asks: request type, what you were looking for, what it should do, where you expected to find it, and your device and browser.

Why something might be missing

● Reason 1 — Circle: tester-only

Newer test material sits behind a testers-only door while the public game stays live. Join the tester list to see it.

■ Reason 2 — Square: not built yet

Some tools are still being made. A request tells us what players want next.

▲ Reason 3 — Triangle: a different name

The thing may exist under another name or in another panel. Say what job you wanted done.

◆ Reason 4 — Diamond: progress-gated

Some quests and tools appear only when your progress makes them meaningful. No spoilers here.

Getting in

Play here: straight into the game. The public live game stays live while newer test material is tested behind the testers-only door.

Tester sign-up: join the official tester list and pick your releases.

Password sign-in stays available. Passkey sign-in is being proven with the owner first, then testers.

Playing and creating

Melody helps in the world, including teaching flight and giving the shrine mission when your progress reaches them. Follow the in-game prompts for controls.

Creation tools grow through testing. If a building tool you want is missing, send a request above and describe the job it should do.

Problems and safety

  • Rule A: report what happened, where, and what you expected.
  • Rule B: include your device and browser.
  • Rule C: never share your password or any sign-in code.
  • Rule D: use the request form for problems too — pick "Something is broken".
